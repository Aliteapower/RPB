## Context

RPB currently operates Reservation, Queue, and Table Management capabilities in one Spring Boot and Vue application. The new Digital Stamp Card product must be an independent, lightweight product line rather than a marketing campaign centre.

Tenants create stamp-card templates. Staff identify or create a tenant Customer by phone, issue a card, append immutable stamp records, and redeem one reward when the target count is reached. Customers use the existing Customer Auth boundary to inspect their own cards and history. The product reuses RPB tenant, store, employee, RBAC, App Gate, product subscription, Customer, audit, internationalisation, business-date, upload, and notification boundaries.

The product does not add stamp-card fields to Reservation, Queue, Table, or Customer tables. Its business data uses the `stamp_card_*` namespace and refers to the shared Customer by `customer_id`.

## Code Audit Summary

The design is based on a read-only audit of the current repository and its adjacent product, Customer, authentication, date, and UI modules.

### Capabilities that already exist

- `platform_apps`, `tenant_product_subscriptions`, `tenant_product_subscription_events`, `tenant_product_subscription_items`, `tenant_app_entitlements`, and `store_app_settings` already provide product registration, tenant subscription, store billing state, entitlement synchronisation, and store enablement.
- `AppGateService` already checks platform product status, tenant entitlement, entitlement expiry, store enablement, actor store access, and a required permission.
- Customer is already tenant-scoped. `customers.phone_e164` is E.164-validated and uniquely indexed by `(tenant_id, phone_e164)` for active records.
- The Customer application and persistence boundaries already support lookup by tenant and phone, Customer creation, and profile maintenance.
- Customer Auth already supports email-code, Google, and Facebook login with tenant-scoped accounts and sessions.
- Reusable audit, business-event, state-transition, idempotency, Store scope, and Store business-time capabilities exist.
- The frontend already has shared country-phone input, store context, Customer lookup, App Gate error handling, Vue Router, Pinia, and `zh-CN` / `en-SG` internationalisation.
- Store configuration uses `DD-MM-YYYY`, and migration `V016__normalize_store_date_format.sql` normalises older date-format values.

### Gaps that must be addressed

- `AppGateService.entryPermissions` is hard-coded to `reservation_queue`. A second product cannot appear in `/api/me/apps` until entry permission discovery is product-aware.
- `PlatformProductLineService` only accepts no route or `/stores/:storeId/staff`; it must accept the controlled stamp-card entry route.
- Product permissions are assigned directly to accounts. The migration must backfill existing accounts, and staff-account creation must assign the new default permissions to future accounts.
- Customer Auth currently creates a Customer when it cannot find an auth account by email; it does not first bind a verified email to an existing Customer. A phone-issued Customer can therefore be disconnected from the Customer portal.
- The shared frontend timestamp helper currently contains an `MM-DD HH:mm` output. Stamp Card must use explicit `DD-MM-YYYY` and `DD-MM-YYYY HH:mm` display helpers.
- The reusable idempotency persistence model exists, but successful replay support must preserve the target and response snapshot for the new commands.
- The current source tree has no `stampcard` bounded context, database tables, APIs, UI routes, or tests.

## Confirmed Decisions

- Implement the product as an independent bounded context inside the existing Spring Boot/Vue deployment.
- Use Java package `com.rpb.reservation.stampcard`; keep `digital_stamp_card` as the external product and App Gate identifier.
- Continue using email-code, Google, and Facebook Customer Auth. V1 does not add SMS OTP.
- Bind a verified email/OAuth identity to exactly one existing active Customer in the same tenant when possible.
- A Customer with only a phone and no bound email may be operated by staff but cannot use the Customer portal until an email is added and verified.
- Freeze required stamp count, reward definition, validity policy, store scope, and content locale after the first template publication.
- After publication, allow only name and description edits plus pause, resume, and archive transitions.
- Permit at most one non-terminal card for the same tenant, Customer, and template. A new card is allowed after the previous card is `REDEEMED`, `EXPIRED`, or `CANCELLED`.
- Allow stamp voiding only before redemption. Voiding preserves the record, requires a reason, decrements the effective count, and moves `REWARD_READY` back to `ACTIVE` when necessary.
- V1 does not reverse a completed redemption.
- Store timestamps in UTC, exchange machine dates in ISO 8601, and display dates as `DD-MM-YYYY` or `DD-MM-YYYY HH:mm`.
- Provide complete Stamp Card copy in `zh-CN` and `en-SG`. Reserve BCP-47 content locales for additional languages without enabling an incomplete platform-wide `zh-TW` UI.

## Goals

1. Register and sell `digital_stamp_card` through the existing product subscription and App Gate model.
2. Let tenant administrators create, publish, pause, resume, archive, and inspect stamp-card templates.
3. Let staff find or create a Customer by E.164 phone and issue a card without duplicating Customer data.
4. Let authorised staff add exactly one stamp per accepted command under concurrent requests.
5. Preserve an append-only stamp history and permit audited pre-redemption voids.
6. Move a full card to `REWARD_READY` and permit exactly one redemption.
7. Let an authenticated Customer read only their own tenant-scoped cards and history.
8. Preserve tenant and store isolation, App Gate enforcement, idempotency, audit, business date, internationalisation, and `DD-MM-YYYY` display behaviour.
9. Deliver the V1 as independently testable vertical slices.

## Non-Goals

V1 excludes:

- online purchase or payment;
- coupons, points, stored value, membership tiers, or marketing campaigns;
- POS integration or automatic consumption detection;
- dynamic QR-code stamping;
- multi-level rewards, recurring cards, or looped missions;
- multi-language merchant-content translation tables;
- SMS OTP or a new customer identity provider;
- redemption reversal;
- automatic stamp expiry batch jobs;
- physical deletion of templates, cards, stamps, redemptions, or audit history;
- a new microservice, Maven module, Redis protocol, message broker, or deployment unit.

## Options Considered

### Selected: bounded context inside the existing monolith

Add an isolated `stampcard` backend package and a lazy-loaded `src/modules/stamp-card` frontend module. Integrate with platform capabilities only through application services and ports.

This is selected because it matches the current deployment, transaction, authentication, and test infrastructure while preserving a boundary that can be extracted if the product later needs independent deployment.

### Rejected: scatter the feature across existing global layers

Adding Stamp Card controllers, service methods, persistence queries, and UI actions directly to Customer, tenant admin, and reservation workbench files would be initially smaller but would make Customer own loyalty rules and would prevent the product from remaining independent.

### Rejected: independent service or Maven deployment module

An independent service would add cross-service identity, transaction, event delivery, observability, and deployment requirements that do not provide V1 value. It conflicts with the product's lightweight positioning.

The structure follows the repository's existing `api/application/domain/persistence` convention. Domain classes must not import Spring, JPA, HTTP, Vue, database entities, or Customer persistence types.

### Dependency direction

```text
stampcard.api
    ↓
stampcard.application
    ↓
stampcard.domain

stampcard.persistence → stampcard.application ports
stampcard customer adapter → Customer application interfaces

```

The Stamp Card module does not access Customer repositories or `customers` with direct SQL. The Customer module exposes explicit query, registration, and verified-email resolution interfaces. A Stamp Card adapter translates those interfaces into Stamp Card Customer gateway records.

The initial `CustomerQueryService`, `CustomerRegistrationService`, and `CustomerContactService` names describe required responsibilities, not permission to depend on internal Customer implementations. Implementation may add those stable Customer application facades or adapt equivalent existing application interfaces, but the Stamp Card boundary consumes only the narrow gateway declared in this document.

### Shared capabilities

- App Gate: product, entitlement, store setting, employee store access, and permission decisions.
- Customer: tenant Customer lookup, registration, profile enrichment, and verified-email resolution.
- Customer Auth: session authentication and verified email/OAuth identity.
- Store: store ownership, active state, timezone, locale, and business date.
- Idempotency: request hash, in-progress state, success snapshot, failure, replay, and conflict.
- Audit: critical operation evidence.
- Business Event: issued, stamped, target reached, redeemed, expired, and stamp voided events.
- Frontend: phone entry, session, store selection, i18n, App Gate errors, and temporal formatting.

## Product Registration and App Gate

### Registration

Register one active platform app:

```text
app_key: digital_stamp_card
app_name: 电子集章卡
default_entry_route: /stores/:storeId/stamp-cards
status: active
```

The migration registers the product but does not create subscriptions, tenant entitlements, or store settings for existing tenants. Platform administrators configure monthly/yearly prices and purchase or open the product through the existing billing workflow.

### Store enablement

The existing store-level subscription item model remains authoritative. When a store item becomes active, the existing entitlement synchronisation creates or enables its `store_app_settings` row. Group tenants can buy for all active stores or selected stores. A single-store tenant uses its only store without a complex selector.

### Runtime decision

An employee operation is allowed only when all conditions are true:

```text
platform app active
AND tenant entitlement enabled or valid trial
AND entitlement not expired
AND current store enabled for the app
AND actor can access the store
AND actor has the endpoint permission
```

The application layer also validates tenant and store ownership. App Gate is not a replacement for aggregate scope checks.

### Product-aware entry permissions

Replace the `reservation_queue` conditional in `AppGateService.entryPermissions` with a product permission catalogue. The catalogue returns stable candidate permissions for each registered product; `entryPermissions(actor, appKey)` then returns only the candidates the actor holds, and an app is visible only when that intersection is nonempty:

```text
reservation_queue → existing Reservation/Queue/Table permissions
digital_stamp_card → all six Stamp Card permissions
```

Unknown products have no visible employee entry. `/api/me/apps` remains the source for product launch visibility.

### Permissions

```text
stamp_card.template.manage
stamp_card.card.issue
stamp_card.card.stamp
stamp_card.card.redeem
stamp_card.record.view
stamp_card.record.void
```

Default account capabilities:

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| `tenant_admin` | all six |
| `store_manager` | issue, stamp, redeem, record view, record void |
| `store_staff` | issue, stamp, redeem |
| Customer | no employee RBAC; authenticated self-read only |

The migration backfills current accounts. Staff-account creation adds the appropriate defaults for accounts created after deployment.

## Domain Model

### StampCardTemplate

`StampCardTemplate` is an aggregate root that owns the issue rules.

Core fields:

```text
templateId
tenantId
name
description
requiredStampCount
rewardDefinition
validityPolicy
storeScope
contentLocale
status
version
```

Statuses:

```text
DRAFT
ACTIVE
PAUSED
ARCHIVED
```

Transitions:

```text
DRAFT → ACTIVE
ACTIVE → PAUSED
PAUSED → ACTIVE
DRAFT | ACTIVE | PAUSED → ARCHIVED
```

Rules:

- Drafts may change all fields.
- Publishing requires a valid target count, nonblank reward title, valid validity policy, valid BCP-47 content locale, and a valid store scope.
- `SELECTED_STORES` requires at least one active store in the same tenant.
- Once published, target count, reward, validity, store scope, and content locale are frozen.
- Name and description remain editable for correction.
- Only `ACTIVE` templates can issue cards.
- `canIssueAt(storeId)` checks `ALL_STORES` or membership in the selected store set.
- Archive is terminal and does not cancel existing cards.

### CustomerStampCard

`CustomerStampCard` is an aggregate root that owns effective progress and redemption readiness.

Core fields:

```text
cardId
tenantId
templateId
customerId
issuedStoreId
issuedBy
issuedAt
validUntil
templateNameSnapshot
requiredStampCount
currentStampCount
rewardSnapshot
contentLocaleSnapshot
status
version
```

Statuses:

```text
ACTIVE
REWARD_READY
REDEEMED
EXPIRED
CANCELLED
```

Rules:

- `ACTIVE` and not expired is required to add a stamp.
- Effective count must remain between zero and the target.
- Reaching the target changes the card to `REWARD_READY` in the same transaction.
- Only `REWARD_READY` may be redeemed.
- Redemption is terminal in V1.
- Voiding a valid stamp before redemption decrements the effective count.
- A void that lowers a `REWARD_READY` card below the target returns it to `ACTIVE`.
- Existing cards use issue-time rule snapshots even when a template name or description is corrected.
- `CANCELLED` remains a supported terminal persistence state for administrative or imported history; V1 exposes no general employee cancellation action.

### StampRecord

`StampRecord` is an append-only ledger record.

Fields:

```text
stampRecordId
tenantId
cardId
sequenceNo
resultingStampCount
storeId
operatorId
stampedAt
businessDate
requestId
status
voidedBy
voidedAt
voidReason
createdAt
```

Statuses:

```text
VALID
VOIDED
```

`sequenceNo` is the immutable append order. It is never reused after a void. `resultingStampCount` is the effective count immediately after the original stamp and provides clear audit evidence when voids cause the current count to diverge from ledger sequence.

A void updates only `status`, `voidedBy`, `voidedAt`, and `voidReason`. No record is deleted or overwritten.

### RedemptionRecord

One `RedemptionRecord` is allowed per card.

Fields:

```text
redemptionId
tenantId
cardId
storeId
operatorId
redeemedAt
businessDate
requestId
createdAt
```

The record is immutable. V1 has no redemption void or reversal state.

### Value objects

- `StampTarget`: integer from 1 through 100.
- `RewardDefinition`: nonblank title and optional description.
- `CardValidityPolicy`: `NO_EXPIRY`, `DAYS_AFTER_ISSUE`, or `FIXED_DATE`.
- `StoreScope`: `ALL_STORES` or a nonempty set of selected store IDs.
- `ContentLocale`: a validated BCP-47 language tag.
- Existing `E164Phone`, `TenantScope`, `StoreScope`, `BusinessDate`, and `IdempotencyKey` remain shared platform values.

For `FIXED_DATE`, the template stores an unformatted calendar date. The card remains valid through that local date and expires at the exclusive start of the following local date in the issuing Store timezone; that instant is stored as UTC `valid_until`. For `DAYS_AFTER_ISSUE`, the card expires at the same local wall-clock time after the configured number of calendar days in the issuing Store timezone, then stores that instant as UTC. All comparisons use the exclusive rule `now >= valid_until`.

## Customer Integration and Authentication

### Stamp Card Customer gateway

The Stamp Card application consumes a narrow gateway:

```text
findActiveByPhone(tenant, phone)
registerByPhone(tenant, phone, displayName, email)
getActiveCustomer(tenant, customerId)
```

The adapter delegates to Customer application interfaces. It returns Customer ID, display name, masked phone, email presence, and portal-link availability. It does not expose Customer entities or repositories.

### Phone issue flow

1. The API accepts an E.164 phone plus optional display name and email.
2. Customer lookup is restricted to the actor tenant.
3. An active match is reused and nonblank profile hints may enrich it through Customer application rules.
4. No match creates one active tenant Customer.
5. The Stamp Card application validates the template and duplicate-card invariant.
6. The card stores only `customer_id`, never a duplicate name, phone, or email field.

### Verified-email binding

After successful email-code or OAuth verification:

1. Reuse an existing active Customer Auth account for the same tenant and normalised email when present.
2. Otherwise query active Customers in the tenant by case-insensitive email.
3. Exactly one match is bound to the new auth account.
4. No match creates a Customer and auth account through the existing auth-registration behaviour.
5. More than one match returns `CUSTOMER_IDENTITY_AMBIGUOUS`; no automatic merge or binding occurs.
6. The Customer session continues to carry tenant ID and Customer ID.

This rule is deliberately narrower than general Customer matching. Phone remains the staff issue identity; only a verified email may claim Customer portal access.

### Customer endpoint security

Customer controllers read the Customer session cookie and authenticate it through Customer Auth. Tenant ID and Customer ID come only from the principal. List and detail queries include both values. A card ID belonging to another Customer or tenant returns not found without revealing ownership.

## Application Flows

### Create or update template

The tenant-admin scope resolver supplies tenant and current Store access. The application validates input, selected Stores, state, and version. Draft updates replace selected Store rows in the same transaction. Published templates reject frozen-field changes with a conflict error.

### Issue card

```text
validate command and App Gate facts
→ start idempotency record
→ find or create Customer through gateway
→ load active template and validate Store scope
→ lock any existing non-terminal card for this Customer/template
→ materialise it as EXPIRED when `now >= valid_until`
→ reject a remaining non-terminal duplicate
→ calculate UTC expiry from Store timezone
→ persist the snapshot card
→ append audit and StampCardIssued event
→ complete idempotency with response snapshot
```

The partial unique index resolves concurrent issue races. Its constraint violation maps to the same duplicate-card conflict. Materialising an expired prior card inside the issue transaction releases the partial-index slot before inserting its replacement.

### Add stamp

```text
validate command and App Gate facts
→ resolve idempotency replay/conflict
→ lock card for update
→ apply effective expiry when necessary
→ validate tenant, Store, template scope, and ACTIVE state
→ allocate max ledger sequence + 1 under the card lock
→ append VALID stamp record
→ increment effective count
→ change to REWARD_READY at the target
→ append audit and events
→ complete idempotency with response snapshot
```

### Redeem reward

```text
validate command and App Gate facts
→ resolve idempotency replay/conflict
→ lock card for update
→ apply effective expiry when necessary
→ require REWARD_READY and an allowed Store
→ insert the unique redemption
→ change card to REDEEMED
→ append audit and StampCardRedeemed
→ complete idempotency with response snapshot
```

### Void stamp

```text
validate nonblank reason and App Gate facts
→ resolve idempotency replay/conflict
→ lock card for update
→ lock target stamp record
→ require VALID record and non-REDEEMED card
→ mark record VOIDED with actor, time, and reason
→ decrement effective count
→ return REWARD_READY to ACTIVE when below target
→ append audit, transition evidence, and StampRecordVoided
→ complete idempotency with response snapshot
```

An `EXPIRED` or `CANCELLED` card remains terminal after a void; only `REWARD_READY` can return to `ACTIVE`.

### Effective expiry

Queries calculate an effective `EXPIRED` status when `now >= valid_until`, even before the stored status has been updated. Issue, stamp, and redemption application transactions return a typed expired outcome instead of throwing a rollback-triggering exception: they persist `EXPIRED` plus audit/event evidence, commit, and the controller then maps the committed outcome to `409 STAMP_CARD_EXPIRED`. V1 does not run an expiry scheduler.

## Transactions, Concurrency, and Idempotency

- Template mutations use optimistic version checks.
- Card issue relies on the partial unique index for concurrent duplicate prevention.
- Stamp, redemption, and void commands load the card using a tenant-scoped pessimistic write lock.
- Sequence allocation occurs only while the card lock is held.
- Redemption also has a unique `card_id` constraint.
- Every command transaction includes business mutation, audit, business events, and idempotency completion. Required audit failure rolls the transaction back.
- `Idempotency-Key` is required for issue, stamp, redeem, and void.
- The request hash covers tenant, Store, actor, target, action, and normalised payload.
- Within the same tenant/Store, source, and action, the same key and same hash replays the saved success response.
- Within that scope, the same key and a different hash returns `STAMP_CARD_IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
- A `started` record returns `STAMP_CARD_COMMAND_IN_PROGRESS`.
- A failed record requires a new key, following current RPB idempotency rules.
- Business-table `request_id` uniqueness remains a defensive constraint and audit link; generic `idempotency_records` owns replay semantics.

## Domain Events and Audit

Events:

```text
StampCardIssued
StampAdded
StampTargetReached
StampCardRedeemed
StampCardExpired
StampRecordVoided
```

V1 writes these to the existing business-event boundary for audit, in-app notification, and future statistics. Domain objects return event intent; they do not send email, WhatsApp, or external messages.

Audit metadata includes:

- actor ID and type;
- tenant and Store scope;
- target type and ID;
- before and after status/count snapshots;
- operation source;
- idempotency key;
- template, Customer, and card references as applicable;
- void reason or failure code.

Critical operations fail closed when required audit evidence cannot be written.

## API Design

All APIs use `/api/v1`, explicit request/response DTOs, stable error codes, and no persistence or domain entity leakage.

Command DTOs are fixed at design level:

| Command | Required request fields | Optional request fields |
|---|---|---|
| create template | `name`, `requiredStampCount`, `reward.title`, `validity.type`, `storeScope.type`, `contentLocale` | `description`, `reward.description`, validity discriminator field, `storeScope.storeIds` |
| update template | `version` plus at least one mutable field | fields from create template subject to lifecycle freezing |
| publish/pause/resume/archive | `version` | none |
| issue card | `templateId`, `phoneE164` | `displayName`, `email` |
| add stamp | `cardVersion` | none |
| redeem | `cardVersion` | none |
| void stamp | `reason`, `cardVersion` | none |

`validity.days` is required only for `DAYS_AFTER_ISSUE`; `validity.fixedDate` is required only for `FIXED_DATE`. `storeScope.storeIds` is required and nonempty only for `SELECTED_STORES`. Command responses return the affected resource ID, current version, current state, relevant count/reward/expiry snapshot, and `idempotentReplay` for idempotent commands. Phone values are normalised and validated as E.164 at the API boundary and again in the Customer application boundary.

## Internationalisation and Date/Time

- New system copy uses the `stampCard.*` namespace.
- `zh-CN` and `en-SG` contain complete labels, state names, actions, confirmations, empty states, permission states, and error keys.
- Backend APIs return error codes and message keys, not fixed Chinese text.
- Merchant content remains single-language in V1 and carries `content_locale`.
- A later multilingual-content extension may add `stamp_card_template_translations(template_id, locale, name, description, reward_title, reward_description)` without changing card progress or redemption rules; this table is not part of V1.
- Database timestamps are `timestamptz` in UTC; business dates are PostgreSQL `date`.
- API dates use ISO 8601, such as `2026-07-20`, solely for machine exchange.
- UI dates display `20-07-2026`.
- UI date-times display `20-07-2026 15:30`.
- Date display uses Store timezone for employee/admin records and the card operation Store timezone supplied by the API for Customer history.
- The implementation adds a tested shared full-date formatter and must not use the current `MM-DD HH:mm` helper for Stamp Card.

## Rollout and Rollback

### Rollout

1. Deploy additive migrations and backend product/App Gate support.
2. Deploy frontend routes and launcher in the same release or after the backend.
3. Configure platform prices.
4. Purchase or open the product only for selected pilot Stores.
5. Validate issue, stamp, void, redemption, and Customer self-read.
6. Expand subscriptions to additional Stores.

No existing tenant receives an automatic entitlement, so deployment alone does not expose the product.

### Rollback

- Hiding or disabling `platform_apps.digital_stamp_card` removes entry visibility without deleting data.
- Subscription or Store setting suspension prevents new operations while retaining history.
- Frontend routes can roll back independently after backend APIs are no longer called.
- Additive business tables remain in place during application rollback; destructive down-migrations are not used.
- Cards, stamps, redemptions, Customer links, and audit evidence created before rollback remain valid history.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Platform administrators can configure and subscribe `digital_stamp_card` for selected Stores.
- Unsubscribed or disabled Stores cannot see or call Stamp Card employee/admin capabilities.
- Tenant administrators can manage templates without changing frozen published rules.
- Staff can find or create a tenant Customer by E.164 phone and issue one non-terminal card per Customer/template.
- Allowed Stores can stamp the same tenant card across Stores; disallowed and cross-tenant Stores cannot.
- Concurrent or repeated commands do not over-stamp, duplicate a card, or redeem twice.
- Every stamp remains in history; an authorised pre-redemption void preserves reason, actor, time, and count correction.
- A full card becomes `REWARD_READY`, and exactly one redemption changes it to `REDEEMED`.
- Verified email/OAuth authentication binds safely to one existing Customer or creates a new Customer when none exists.
- Customer endpoints return only the authenticated Customer's tenant cards and never expose write operations.
- New system copy is complete in `zh-CN` and `en-SG`.
- Every Stamp Card UI date is `DD-MM-YYYY`; every Stamp Card UI timestamp is `DD-MM-YYYY HH:mm`.
- Required domain, migration, application, API, security, frontend, build, and runtime acceptance checks pass.
